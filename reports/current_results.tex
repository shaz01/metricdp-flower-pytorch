\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption*{Round-matched AUC (0.5 = no leakage, 1.0 = perfect leakage), pooled across 3 seeds.

\end{table}

\noindent
Leakage is highest for \code{homogeneous/vanilla} (0.727); both DP mechanisms reduce it there,
and \code{non-iid} shows much weaker leakage across every privacy mode, including one AUC
(non-iid/metric-privacy, noisy score) that falls below the 0.5 no-leakage line. All confidence
intervals span roughly 0.30--0.35 AUC units and overlap 0.5, so these should be read as a
directional pattern rather than a statistically confirmed ranking.
