\noindent
\code{metric-privacy} distance metric: for every pair of sampled client models, compute the
Euclidean (L2) distance between each corresponding layer, then average across layers to get one
scalar per pair; $d$ is the \emph{maximum} of these mean layer-wise distances over all client
pairs in the round. Noise scale that round is \code{noise\_multiplier}$/d$ (vs.\ \code{global-dp}'s
flat \code{noise\_multiplier}, independent of client-model spread).
