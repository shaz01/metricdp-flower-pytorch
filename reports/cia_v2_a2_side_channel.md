## 1. Question

Metric privacy scales its noise by a client distance, so the noise level itself depends on who takes part. An
observer can estimate the noise std from one released update to about `1/sqrt(2D)` relative precision, whatever its
size (REVIEW.md Section 5).

The hypothesis: in small federations, metric privacy's noise level separates the worlds with and without the largest
client even at noise strong enough to silence the loss attacker under global DP.

## 2. Protocol

**Cells and targets.** Label-Dirichlet alpha 0.3, layout seed 42, 50 rounds, equal weights, WP1's data modules and
hyperparameters. The target is each cell's largest client:

| Cell | Client sizes | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Alzheimer, 3 clients | 1,121 / 1,333 / 2,666 | client 2 |
| Alzheimer, 5 clients | 302 / 2,328 / 1,622 / 138 / 730 | client 1 |
| Alzheimer, 8 clients | 236 / 253 / 407 / 307 / 440 / 1,891 / 1,040 / 546 | client 5 |
| EuroSAT, 3 clients | 3,582 / 9,573 / 8,445 | client 1 |

**Worlds.** Removal adjacency: IN (all clients) and OUT (target removed). Every trajectory has its own `client_seed`
and `noise_seed` from `SeedSequence([3, cell, world, replicate])`; no two worlds share private randomness.

**Mechanisms.** Global DP and the paper's metric privacy, with noise std independent of the active count.

**Pilot, per cell.** Global DP, 4 replicates per world, at 1, 4, 16 and 64 times WP1's base ratio (EuroSAT 0.001546,
Alzheimer 0.01/3), in increasing order. The cell's level is the first at which the calibrated loss attacker's point
AUC is 0.6 or below; with none up to 64x the cell gets no main runs. Pilot trajectories are never reused.

**Main runs, per qualifying cell.** Global DP and metric privacy at the chosen level, `R = 8` per world.

**Attackers.**

- Calibrated loss attacker on the target's shadow loss at rounds 0, 5, ..., 50.
- Blind noise-level attacker: per round `sigma_hat_t = ||Delta_t|| / sqrt(D)` from the released-update norm; primary
  feature its mean over rounds, the 50-round vector as a sensitivity check.
- Oracle: the strategy's logged true noise std.

**Interval.** Pair jackknife at 97.5%, chosen by simulation at A2's size (one target, 8 pairs). With 16 trials the
AUC is too discrete for any per-cell interval to hold 5% just below 0.8 (up to 7.9% at an expected AUC of 0.787).
Over two of four cells the gate's false-claim probability is about 3.4%.

**Gate G-A2** (ROADMAP.md): "a strong claim holds if metric privacy's noise-level AUC is 0.8 or higher at the lower
interval end while global DP's loss AUC is 0.6 or below in at least two cells. Otherwise record the size of the
channel at the tested noise." The global-DP condition is read on the main runs' point estimate.

**Cost rule.** The cost per trajectory is re-measured after the first pilot level. If the projected total exceeds the
35 GPU-h cap by more than 25% (43.75 GPU-h), the package stops and escalates.

## 3. First escalation: cost

The 1x pilot (32 trajectories, one worker) took 0.40-0.45 h per trajectory, against the planned 0.07 for Alzheimer.
Small federations leave the GPU nearly idle (1-2% utilization) and were CPU-bound on a machine loaded by other users.
No cell qualified at 1x, and the projected total was at least about 83 GPU-h: the stop rule triggered.

**Owner's resolution** (option 1 of four): run 4 workers per GPU, re-measure on the 4x pilot, and continue only if the
projection falls under 43.75 GPU-h.

The 4x pilot took 3.84 GPU-h of occupancy for 32 trajectories, about 0.12 GPU-h each (about 0.10 for Alzheimer and
0.16 for EuroSAT). The projection fell to 34.3-36.4 GPU-h, so A2 continued.

## 5. Second escalation: collapsed rounds

After the 16x pilot and the main runs at 4x, the analysis stopped on rounds with no released-update norm. In those
**collapsed rounds** every client's update had zero norm (total divergence); the strategy skipped aggregation, kept the
previous model and released nothing (`metricdp_pytorch/globaldp_strategy.py`, `metricdp_strategy.py`).

| Cell and level | Trajectories with collapsed rounds | Collapsed rounds | Mean accuracy IN / OUT |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alzheimer 8, global DP 16x (pilot) | 8 of 8, from round 3-5 on | 370 of 400 | 0.247 / 0.250 |
| Alzheimer 3, metric privacy 4x (main) | 5 of 16 | 16 of 800 | 0.755 / 0.461 |
| Alzheimer 5, metric privacy 4x (main) | 8 of 16, from round 24 on | 141 of 800 | 0.665 / 0.331 |

Alzheimer 8 "qualified" at 16x only because its federation was dead, and EuroSAT 3 still leaked fully at 16x with
accuracy 0.438.

**Owner's amendment** (PROTOCOL.md Section 6, made before any main-run statistic):

1. A collapsed round's released norm and true noise std are 0, since an observer of the released models sees the model
   unchanged. The loss feature is unaffected.
2. A pilot level with any collapsed global-DP trajectory does not qualify, and the cell gets no main runs.
3. No further runs: EuroSAT 3's 64x pilot is not run, and A2 is analyzed on Alzheimer 3 and 5.

`analyze.py` implements the amendment with 3 new tests (20 A2 tests pass; full suite 742 passed, 4 skipped).

## 6. Main result: channel size recorded

Main runs at 4x, 8 replicates per world. Intervals: pair jackknife at 97.5% (it can leave [0, 1] at this size).

| Cell | Mechanism | Loss AUC | Final-round loss AUC | Noise-level AUC (mean `sigma_hat`) | 50-round noise-level AUC | Oracle noise AUC | Accuracy IN / OUT | Collapsed trajectories (rounds) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alzheimer 3 | global DP | 0.047 (-0.105-0.199) | 0.062 | 0.281 (-0.066-0.628) | 0.375 | 0.500 | 0.476 / 0.427 | 0 |
| Alzheimer 3 | metric privacy | 0.984 (0.918-1.051) | 0.875 | **1.000 (1.000-1.000)** | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.755 / 0.461 | 5 (16) |
| Alzheimer 5 | global DP | 0.672 (0.242-1.102) | 0.906 | 0.516 (0.007-1.024) | 0.234 | 0.500 | 0.427 / 0.359 | 0 |
| Alzheimer 5 | metric privacy | 1.000 (1.000-1.000) | 1.000 | 0.875 (0.520-1.230) | 1.000 | 0.906 | 0.665 / 0.331 | 8 (141) |

**G-A2: channel size recorded.** Alzheimer 3 meets both conditions as written; Alzheimer 5 meets neither (noise-level
lower end 0.520; global-DP loss AUC 0.672). The strong claim needs two cells.

**Alzheimer 3's pass is an attacker artifact.** One IN trajectory under global DP diverged (final loss 63.9, against
1.1-1.5 for the others). It sits in every leave-pair-out reference set but its own, so the calibrated attacker learns
"IN has higher loss" and scores 0.047. A plain final-round loss threshold on the same runs gives 0.797 in Alzheimer 3
and 0.969 in Alzheimer 5. A null simulation at A2's size gives a mean calibrated AUC of 0.45-0.47, and a value as low
as 0.047 in about 2-4% of cells.

## 7. Reading

- **Metric privacy's noise level is observable.** The blind attacker separates IN from OUT almost perfectly: 1.000
  and 0.875 on the round mean, 1.000 on the 50-round vector. Under global DP, whose noise is fixed, the oracle is at
  chance (0.500).
- **At the same noise multiple metric privacy also leaks through the loss** (AUC 0.984 and 1.000), because it keeps
  far more accuracy (IN 0.755 and 0.665 against 0.476 and 0.427 for global DP) and so adds much less noise. The side
  channel is not its only leak, nor its largest. A1 compares the mechanisms at equal accuracy instead.
- **Global DP still leaks at 4x** in both cells, by the plain threshold. The calibrated attacker's failure there also
  weakens the pilot decisions it made at 4x (0.375, 0.563, 0.625).
- **The noise that silences global DP breaks training.** Wherever global DP's attacker drops at all, Alzheimer
  accuracy is 0.43-0.50, about the majority-class rate (Non_Demented is 50.1% of the training split); Alzheimer 8 at 16x collapses to chance (0.247); EuroSAT 3
  leaks fully at 16x with accuracy 0.438.
- **Collapse is part of metric privacy's channel.** 13 of its 32 main trajectories collapse in late rounds, and an
  observer sees those rounds as unchanged models.

## 8. Anomalies and corrections

- **Cost blow-up** at 1x (CPU-bound small federations): escalated, resolved by 4 workers per GPU.
- **Collapsed rounds** crashed the fixed analysis: escalated, resolved by the owner's amendment.
- **Attacker artifact** from one diverged trajectory: found while checking the 0.047, reported in FINDINGS. It is not
  corrected in the gate, which is applied as written.
- **Disk:** the server's root disk was 97% full (about 62 GB free) throughout, from other users' data; the job
  framework warned on every run, and no run failed.

## 9. Caveats

- Two analyzed cells, 8 replicates per world, one target per cell, one layout seed.
- The mechanisms are compared at equal noise multiples, not at matched accuracy.
- The calibrated attacker is not robust to diverged trajectories, which matters wherever global DP's noise approaches
  collapse.
- The amendment was made after the runs but before any main-run statistic.
- Per-cell interval statements carry the up to 8% boundary error of a 16-trial AUC.

## 11. Conclusions and owner decisions

1. **G-A2: channel size recorded.** Metric privacy's noise level is visible to a blind observer (AUC 1.000 and 0.875
   in two small Alzheimer federations), but the strong claim fails: one qualifying cell, and that cell's global-DP
   condition holds only through an attacker artifact.
2. **Owner decisions:** 4 workers per GPU after the cost escalation (2026-10-18); the collapsed-round amendment and no
   further runs (2026-10-19).
3. **For the audit claim:** the side channel is real but secondary. At equal noise, metric privacy leaks fully through
   the loss as well; at equal accuracy (A1), its leakage matches global DP's.
4. **Closed by the owner** on 2026-10-20 together with M0, M1, M2 and A1.
