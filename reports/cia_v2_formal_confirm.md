# CIA roadmap v2, B4 confirmatory run: the scarce-public positive on a fresh layout

Owner decision 11 (`research/cia_roadmap_v2/ROADMAP.md` Section 10, 2026-10-20), branch
`feature/cia-v2-formal-confirm`. The owner closed it on 2026-10-23.

All numbers below come from committed artifacts in `results/cia_v2/formal_regime/confirm/`: `PROTOCOL.md` (fixed before
any run), `STATE.md`, `FINDINGS.md` and `analysis.json`, regenerated by `python -m experiments.frozen_head.confirm` from
the 480 runs in `results/cia_v2/formal_regime/results/confirm_public/`. B4 itself is reported in
`reports/cia_v2_formal_regime.md`.

## 1. Question

B4 (Agent C) failed its gate: with a 1,350-image public set, head-only DP-FedAvg on frozen ResNet-18 features gains at
most 3.49 pp over the public-only head at `mu_total <= 8`. Its owner-requested exploration (Amendments 1 and 2) found a
conditional positive: with 20-100 public images, the DP head at `mu_total <= 2` beats the strongest legitimate
public-only baseline by 5-26 pp. That finding was made after B4's pre-registered result was known, on one evaluation
layout (42) with one set of public subsets. This run asks whether it holds on fresh data.

## 2. Protocol

- **Unchanged from B4:** model (frozen ResNet-18 ImageNet features, linear head), federations (`n` = 48 and 480),
  mechanism (client-level DP-FedAvg, exact GDP `mu_total = sqrt(T) / z`), settings, hyperparameters (B4's Part A tuning
  selections per public size, from tuning layout 43) and the clip base norms.
- **New:** evaluation layout 44, its own public subsets 0-4 at 20 and 50 images (`default_rng([44, size, subset])`),
  and replicate seeds `client_seed = 30000 + j`, `noise_seed = 40000 + j`.
- **Settings:** the 8 with `mu_total <= 2` and the 4 no-noise references, per size: 2 x 12 x 5 subsets x 4 replicates
  = 480 runs.
- **Gain:** mean over subsets of (mean DP-head accuracy minus that subset's public-only accuracy) on layout 44's
  1,350-image server test set; 95% bootstrap over subsets, replicates and test images.
- **Baseline:** `best_legit`, per subset the most accurate of four legitimate public-only heads chosen on the test set
  (which favours the baseline); `protocol` and `oracle_c` reported alongside.
- **Confirmation rule (fixed before the runs):** per size, "confirmed" if every setting that was robust against
  best_legit in B4's sweep (7 at 20 images, 6 at 50) again has a lower interval end of at least 5 pp; "partly
  confirmed" if some do; "not confirmed" if none.

## 4. Reading

- **The positive replicates closely.** Every gain is within about 2.5 pp of the original and slightly larger, because
  layout 44's public-only heads are a little weaker (0.627 and 0.762 against 0.660 and 0.782).
- **The settings that were not robust stay not robust:** `n = 48`, `mu = 1` at 50 images (4.62 and 2.53). At 20 images,
  `n = 48`, `T = 20`, `mu = 1` now clears 5 pp (11.05 [7.33, 15.43]); it was not part of the rule.
- **What it establishes:** for this setup (EuroSAT, frozen ResNet-18 ImageNet features, a linear head, Dirichlet 0.3),
  a formal client-level guarantee with an AUC bound of at most 0.921 (`mu <= 2`) and useful accuracy coexist when the
  public set is very small (2-5 images per class). With a 1,350-image public set, the public-only model wins (B4).

## 5. Caveats

- The `n = 480` rows protect pseudo-clients; for the 48 real clients they imply `10 mu` by group privacy.
- Hyperparameters were tuned on private records of layout 43, which is not counted in the privacy budget (as in B4).
- One extractor, one dataset, one partition; 5 public subsets per size, and the subset term dominates the intervals.
- No empirical audit at layout 44 (B4's audits at layout 42 found the realistic attacker far below the formal bound).
