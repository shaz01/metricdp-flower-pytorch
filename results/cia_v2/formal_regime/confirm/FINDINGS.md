## Verdict

**Confirmed at both public sizes.** On a fresh evaluation layout (44), fresh public subsets and fresh replicate seeds,
every setting that was robust against the strongest legitimate public-only baseline in B4's sweep is robust again:
7 of 7 at 20 public images and 6 of 6 at 50.

B4's scarce-public statement therefore holds as a confirmed result for this setup: **with 2-5 public images per class,
head-only DP-FedAvg on frozen ResNet-18 features at `mu_total <= 2` beats the best legitimate public-only head by
14-26 pp at 20 images and 10-14 pp at 50 (the 13 settings in bold below), at a meaningful formal guarantee.**

## Reading

- **The positive replicates closely.** Every gain is within about 2.5 pp of the original, and slightly larger here,
  because layout 44's public-only heads are a little weaker (0.627 and 0.762 mean against 0.660 and 0.782 on layout 42
  with the protocol head).
- **The two settings that were not robust are not robust here either.** `n = 48`, `mu = 1` at 50 images (4.62 and
  2.53) stays below 5 pp. At 20 images, `n = 48`, `T = 20`, `mu = 1` now clears 5 pp (11.05 [7.33, 15.43]); it was
  not part of the rule.
- **Scope.** This confirms the exploration statement for this setup: EuroSAT, frozen ResNet-18 ImageNet features, a
  linear head, Dirichlet 0.3. The `n = 480` rows protect pseudo-clients (`10 mu` per real client by group privacy).
  Hyperparameters were tuned on private records of layout 43, which is not counted in the privacy budget, as in B4.
