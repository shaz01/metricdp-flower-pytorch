2Behavior of the calibration distance d(r)

All quantities logged server-side per round. d(r) = max pairwise layer-averaged Frobenius distance between local models; noise σ = nε·C / (nc·d(r)).

3Utility across client scale and noise level

CIFAR-10 restricted to 4 classes, non-i.i.d. partitions · FedAvg · noise ratio = nm / nc · 3 seeds (48 clients: 1 seed).

4Client-inference attack, multi-round protocol

Attacker score sr = −loss of round-r global model on a 10% shadow split of the target, perturbed with Gaussian pixel noise (σ = 20% max value). AUC computed IN vs. OUT over 20 rounds × 3 seeds (60 vs. 60 scores), 95% bootstrap CIs.
