# Broader oracle diagnostic: frozen protocol

2026-10-05. Owner: “lets move to that”, then “continue”. Approved question: does a broader parameter subspace give material full-information personalization headroom before a private estimator is worth building? This is a bounded research calculation, not a final mechanism or CIA experiment.

This protocol and calculator are written before reading the fresh evaluation outcomes. They are local research records, not an external preregistration.

Reuse the saved Fashion-MNIST fixed-feature 68-parameter heads at rounds 5 and 20, three seeds and three existing partition regimes. Fixed orthonormal Helmert class contrasts remove common-logit gauge directions. Spaces: all three bias contrasts, or all 51 identifiable head directions (three contrasts times seventeen feature/bias coordinates). The latter spans every predictive change in this reduced classifier, while the former freezes feature weights.

In each space use four public coordinate L1 bodies: equal axes, or one retained class-contrast group with other groups compressed to 0.25. Common radius grid: 0.001, 0.005, 0.02, 0.1, 0.3, 1, 3. Noise labels 4, 8, 16; fixed-profile independent coordinate Laplace scale 2r/label. These labels are counterfactual calibrations, not privacy guarantees for the raw trajectory or selections. This is a broader analogue of the earlier diamond bank, not the identical two-dimensional rotated bank.

Both shared and personalized diagnostics see the same raw client fit updates and global development loss derivatives. Shared chooses one body and common radius. Oracle exactly enumerates 4^8 client assignments per radius and contains every shared assignment. Both optimize common postprocessing shrink t in [0,1], scaling **both mean and noise**. Development objective is L t + (Q+N) t², L=gᵀm, Q=mᵀHm/2, N=tr(H Σ)/2; optimum clip(−L/[2(Q+N)],0,1). No intervention is included at t=0. Neither diagnostic is deployable privacy-accounted selection. Record raw/noiseless, clipped-unshrunk, clipped-shrunk and noise controls and aggregate clipping deviation.

Fresh evaluation: official test examples 256:512 within each class, disjoint from previous 0:256 subset, fixed across seeds. 1,024 paired fresh noise draws per arm and cell. No evaluation-driven setting changes or extra grid searches after this run. Expected 36 space/checkpoint/partition/seed rows and 108 epsilon comparisons. Gate: oracle improvement at least 0.001 absolute cross-entropy over strongest shared choice, reported separately for point estimates and conditional noise-only 95% normal intervals. A robust follow-up opportunity requires repeatable benefit across seeds within the same regime/space/budget, not one favorable cell. The same criterion is retained, not lowered after inspecting outputs.

Source Arrow hashes must match the saved trajectory. Checks: orthonormality/gauge, projected finite-difference Hessian, L1 containment, analytic versus directly reconstructed score, no-op inclusion, oracle includes shared, direct loss identity, fresh test separation, and paired trial summaries. Source images remain local. Trial losses/indices/bases are committed for audit. No CNN, Flower or CIA run is authorized by this diagnostic.

Limits: finite profile bank and common radii; quadratic development selection; raw source checkpoints; same training corpus and repeated test examples across seeds; noise-only exploratory intervals without multiplicity correction; no DP, general client-population, novel density or attack-efficacy claim. Full 51-dimensional head removes the earlier coordinate restriction only for this fixed-feature classifier, not for full CNN learning.
